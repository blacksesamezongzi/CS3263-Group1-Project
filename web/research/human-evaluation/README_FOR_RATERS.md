# Independent course-plan rating

Open `human_evaluation_packet.pdf` and read the task, rubric and minor supplement. Use `course_reference.csv`, `task_supplement.json` and `context.json` as common reference material.

Identify yourself as **R1** or **R2**. Fill only your 15 rows of `blank_ratings.csv`, in the order listed for your rater ID. Every dimension uses an integer from 1 to 5; the PDF defines every level. Leave a score blank if you cannot judge it, and explain the uncertainty. Please note a specific course or semester concern where applicable.

Work independently. Do not discuss scores, view model outputs or checker verdicts, open the method key, or guess the producing method. Record whether you recognise a plan from prior work. Return your own completed copy. There is no preferred answer and no requirement to favour any method.

The schedule pages deliberately use identical formatting and omit generated explanations. Feasibility ratings are your assessments from the common references, not official graduation certification. Future course availability and CourseReg eligibility still require confirmation.
